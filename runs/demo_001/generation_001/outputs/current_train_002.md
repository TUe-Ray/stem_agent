# Summary  
To organize your messy project notes, the best approach is to systematically gather, sort, categorize, and digitize them for easy access and future reference.

# Concrete Steps  
1. **Gather all notes**: Collect all project notes scattered across notebooks, loose papers, digital files, emails, and apps.  
2. **Sort by source and date**: Arrange them roughly by their source (e.g., meeting notes, brainstorming, research) and chronological order.  
3. **Categorize by topics/themes**: Identify main topics or themes within your project, and classify notes accordingly. Use tags or labels if digital.  
4. **Digitize physical notes**: Scan or photograph paper notes to create digital copies, using apps like Evernote, OneNote, or Notion.  
5. **Consolidate digital notes**: Import or copy all digital notes into one organized platform or folder structure.  
6. **Create an index or summary document**: Make a master outline or table of contents referencing key notes to quickly find information.  
7. **Maintain regularly**: Set a recurring schedule (weekly or after meetings) to update and organize notes, preventing future messiness.

# Final Answer  
To organize your messy project notes effectively, first gather all notes together and sort them by source and date. Next, categorize them based on key project themes. Digitize any paper notes so everything is in one digital system like Evernote or Notion. Then, create an index or summary document for easy navigation. Finally, commit to regular maintenance of your organized notes to keep them manageable going forward.
