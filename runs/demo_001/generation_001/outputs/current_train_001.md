# Morning Routine Planning

## Summary
A simple morning routine helps start the day positively, improves focus, and boosts energy. The key is to include a few manageable, consistent steps that prepare your mind and body for the day ahead.

## Concrete Steps to Plan Your Morning Routine
1. **Wake Up Consistently**  
   Set a fixed wake-up time every day to regulate your internal clock.

2. **Hydrate**  
   Drink a glass of water shortly after waking to rehydrate your body.

3. **Stretch or Light Exercise**  
   Spend 5-10 minutes doing gentle stretches or light exercise to wake your muscles.

4. **Personal Hygiene**  
   Brush your teeth, wash your face, and use the restroom to refresh yourself.

5. **Healthy Breakfast**  
   Eat a simple, nutritious breakfast to fuel your body.

6. **Plan Your Day Briefly**  
   Spend 2-3 minutes reviewing your priorities or goals to set your mindset.

7. **Optional: Mindfulness or Meditation**  
   If time allows, spend 5 minutes on mindfulness or deep breathing to reduce stress.

## Final Answer: Example Simple Morning Routine  
- 7:00 AM: Wake up  
- 7:05 AM: Drink a glass of water  
- 7:10 AM: Stretch or light exercise  
- 7:20 AM: Personal hygiene  
- 7:30 AM: Healthy breakfast  
- 7:45 AM: Review daily priorities  
- 7:50 AM: Optional meditation  

This routine takes under an hour and sets a positive tone for the day without being overwhelming.
