### Summary
Organizing messy project notes improves clarity, productivity, and ease of access. A systematic approach to sorting, categorizing, and digitizing your notes can transform chaos into a practical knowledge base.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Gather All Notes**  
   Collect all your project notes from different places—physical notebooks, loose papers, digital files, emails, apps.

2. **Sort and Categorize**  
   Group notes by topics, project phases, dates, or any logical categories relevant to your project.

3. **Decide on a Format**  
   Choose how you want to keep your organized notes—for example, digital documents, note-taking apps (e.g., Notion, Evernote), or a structured physical notebook.

4. **Digitize Physical Notes**  
   Scan or type handwritten notes, making them searchable and easier to organize.

5. **Create a Clear Structure**  
   Use folders, tags, headings, or a table of contents to map out your notes in the chosen format.

6. **Summarize and Highlight Key Points**  
   Rewrite or annotate notes to underline crucial information and action items.

7. **Set Up a Maintenance Routine**  
   Regularly update and review your notes to keep them organized and relevant.

### Final Answer
To organize your messy project notes effectively, start by collecting all notes in one place, then sort them into meaningful categories. Choose a format for storing these notes—digital platforms like Notion or Evernote are recommended for easy search and editing. Digitize any physical notes for better management. Structure your notes with clear folders, tags, and summaries to enhance clarity. Finally, maintain your organized system by scheduling regular reviews and updates. This approach will make your project information accessible, actionable, and easy to maintain.
