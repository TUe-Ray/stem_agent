### Summary
To effectively organize your messy project notes, you need to centralize all notes, categorize them systematically, apply a consistent naming system, and establish a routine for maintenance and backups. This approach increases accessibility and improves your productivity.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Gather All Notes**  
   Collect all your project notes from various sources such as notebooks, files, emails, and apps into one central location.

2. **Select an Organization Tool**  
   Choose a tool that fits your style and needs (e.g., Notion, OneNote, Evernote, Google Docs, or a structured folder system).

3. **Categorize and Tag Notes**  
   Sort notes by relevant categories like project phase, topic, or priority. Use tags or keywords for easier searching.

4. **Apply Consistent Naming Conventions**  
   Rename notes with a clear format, such as “ProjectName_Topic_Date,” to maintain uniformity and ease of identification.

5. **Summarize Each Note and Highlight Action Items**  
   Write a short summary for each note and bold or highlight key tasks, decisions, or deadlines.

6. **Establish a Regular Maintenance Schedule**  
   Set weekly or biweekly reviews to update, reorganize, and clean your notes to avoid clutter.

7. **Backup Your Organized Notes**  
   Use cloud storage or external drives to backup your notes regularly and prevent data loss.

### Final Answer
Organize your messy project notes by consolidating them into one central tool or folder, categorizing and tagging for clarity, using a consistent naming scheme, and summarizing key points. Maintain this system with regular reviews and secure your data through regular backups to keep your notes orderly and accessible.
