### Summary
This simple morning routine helps you start each day with energy, focus, and a positive mindset by incorporating hydration, movement, mindfulness, nutrition, and planning.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Wake up consistently:** Choose a specific time (e.g., 7:00 AM) to wake up every day to regulate your internal clock.  
2. **Hydrate:** Drink a glass of water as soon as you get out of bed to rehydrate your body.  
3. **Move your body:** Spend 5-10 minutes stretching or doing light exercise to boost circulation and wake up your muscles.  
4. **Practice mindfulness:** Dedicate 3-5 minutes to meditation, deep breathing, or gratitude to calm your mind and center your focus.  
5. **Eat a healthy breakfast:** Have a balanced meal with protein, fiber, and healthy fats to fuel your morning.  
6. **Set priorities:** Review or write down your top 3 tasks to focus on for the day, organizing your time effectively.

### Final Answer
Adopt a morning routine where you wake up at a consistent time, drink water, do light exercises, practice mindfulness, eat a nutritious breakfast, and set clear priorities for the day. This routine is simple to follow and helps create momentum for a productive and balanced day.
