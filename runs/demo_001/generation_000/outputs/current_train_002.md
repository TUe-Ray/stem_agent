### Summary
To organize your messy project notes effectively, you should collect all notes, categorize them, choose an organization system (digital or physical), digitize if needed, and implement tagging and summaries for easy access. Regular maintenance ensures the system remains useful over time.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Gather all notes:** Collect every piece of your project notes, including physical papers, notebooks, and digital files.
2. **Sort by category:** Group notes by topics such as ideas, research, tasks, or deadlines.
3. **Select your organization tool:** Choose a digital platform (Notion, Evernote, OneNote) or physical method (folders, binders).
4. **Digitize non-digital notes:** Scan or photograph handwritten notes to create searchable digital versions.
5. **Create a clear structure:** Set up folders, notebooks, or sections based on your categories.
6. **Use tags and keywords:** Label notes with relevant tags to facilitate quick searching and filtering.
7. **Summarize key points:** Write brief summaries per category to capture main ideas and next steps.
8. **Set a maintenance schedule:** Regularly review and update your notes system weekly or biweekly to keep it current.

### Final Answer
Organize your messy project notes by gathering all materials and sorting them into meaningful categories. Choose either a digital or physical system to create a consistent, accessible structure, digitize handwritten notes, and apply tags along with summaries for quick reference. Finally, maintain your organized system regularly to keep it effective and up-to-date.
