### Summary
Organizing messy project notes improves productivity and clarity. The process involves categorizing, digitizing, summarizing, and maintaining notes systematically.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Gather All Notes**  
   Collect all scattered notes (paper, digital files, emails, etc.) into one place.

2. **Sort and Categorize**  
   Group notes by topic, project phase, priority, or type (e.g., ideas, research, tasks).

3. **Choose a Note-Taking or Organization Tool**  
   Select a digital tool such as Notion, Evernote, OneNote, or simple folders with consistent naming for easier search and retrieval.

4. **Digitize Physical Notes**  
   Scan or photograph handwritten notes and upload them to the chosen tool.

5. **Create a Structured System**  
   Define folders, tags, or notebooks according to categories. Use headings, bullet points, and links to interconnect notes.

6. **Summarize and Highlight Key Information**  
   For each note or group, write a brief summary or extract key points to improve quick reference later.

7. **Set Regular Review and Update Times**  
   Schedule weekly or biweekly sessions to revise and update your notes, keeping the organization current.

### Final Answer
To organize your messy project notes effectively, start by gathering them all together, then sort them into meaningful categories. Use a digital tool suited to your workflow and digitize physical notes for consolidation. Build a clear folder/tag structure and add summaries or highlights to each note. Finally, maintain your system with regular reviews to ensure notes stay organized and useful.
