### Summary
A simple morning routine sets a positive tone for the day by combining healthy habits that boost energy, improve focus, and create calmness. Consistency and balance between physical care, nutrition, and mental preparation are key.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Set a Consistent Wake-Up Time:** Decide on a fixed time to wake up daily (e.g., 6:30 AM) to align your body's internal clock.  
2. **Hydrate Immediately:** Drink a glass of water within 10 minutes of waking to replenish fluids.  
3. **Engage in Light Physical Activity:** Spend 5-10 minutes stretching or doing gentle exercises to wake up your body.  
4. **Complete Personal Hygiene:** Brush your teeth, wash your face, and shower if preferred.  
5. **Eat a Nutritious Breakfast:** Include protein, healthy fats, and fiber-rich foods for lasting energy.  
6. **Practice Mindfulness or Plan Your Day:** Take 5 minutes to meditate, journal, or set daily goals to foster mental clarity.  
7. **Prepare to Transition:** Gather what you need, dress appropriately, and review your schedule to shift into productivity mode.

### Final Answer
Here is an example of a simple morning routine you can follow:

- **6:30 AM:** Wake up  
- **6:35 AM:** Drink a glass of water  
- **6:40 AM:** Stretch or do light exercise for 5-10 minutes  
- **6:50 AM:** Brush teeth, wash face, and shower  
- **7:10 AM:** Eat a healthy breakfast  
- **7:30 AM:** Spend 5 minutes on mindfulness or planning  
- **7:40 AM:** Prepare for the day and get dressed  

This routine is easy to implement, balances important areas of well-being, and can help you start your mornings feeling energized and focused.
