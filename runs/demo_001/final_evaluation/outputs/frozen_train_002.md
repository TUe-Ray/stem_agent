### Summary
You want to effectively organize your messy project notes to enhance clarity, accessibility, and productivity.

### Concrete Steps
1. **Gather all notes:** Collect every project note from all sources—digital files, physical notebooks, emails, and apps—into one place.
2. **Sort notes by themes or categories:** Group notes based on topics, project stages, or related tasks to create logical clusters.

6. **Summarize key points and action items:** Edit each note to highlight important information and next steps for quick reference.
